## About

I am a final-year **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student** focused on building production-oriented software systems at the intersection of **software engineering, artificial intelligence, and research**.

My work spans **full-stack development, AI/ML, computer vision, multimodal AI, RAG systems, LLM experimentation, and intelligent product engineering**. I enjoy taking an idea from a research hypothesis or product requirement through architecture, implementation, evaluation, and deployment.

I approach engineering with a strong emphasis on:

- **Software Engineering** — scalable architectures, clean APIs, maintainable systems, and reliable developer workflows.
- **AI / ML** — computer vision, NLP, multimodal learning, RAG, LLMs, model evaluation, and experimentation.
- **Full-Stack Development** — React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, FastAPI, databases, and cloud deployment.
- **Product Engineering** — transforming technical capabilities into usable, measurable products rather than isolated prototypes.
- **Research Engineering** — designing experiments, benchmarking systems, analyzing failure modes, and translating findings into reproducible implementations.
